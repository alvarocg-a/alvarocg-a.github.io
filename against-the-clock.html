About the game

Against The Clock is a simple game where you have to collect all the hammers on the map and go through a portal before the clock hits 00:00.

This project was my first Unreal Engine game, inspired by Carlos Coronado's Unreal Engine 4 course.

My contributions

This was a solo project for my BSc in Game Design and Development, so I was in charge of all the design, programming and art.
