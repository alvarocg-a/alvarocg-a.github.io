Blinkshot · Master's Degree Final Project · 2023

Bugs 'N' Guns

A 3rd-person co-op shooter where players take on the role of interplanetary garbage disposers. Their task is to escort a train through a hostile alien environment, using elemental weapons and combinations to fight against a mysterious race of insects.

I worked as Game Designer and Technical Designer on this game as my Master's Degree Final Project.
