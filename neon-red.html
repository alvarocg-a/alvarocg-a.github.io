About the game

Neon Red is a platformer for speedrunners. Using the red line connecting your arm and hand, you'll need to use momentum and swings to reach the goal as quickly as possible. Defeat enemies and strive to improve your times.

Are you ready to cross the red line and enter Neon Red?

I worked as Game Designer and Programmer on this game at the Com JamOn.

My contributions

Designed and programmed (all in Blueprints) all the mechanics and systems, including, among others:

  • Player core mechanics
    • The movement and control mechanics of the player.
    • The grappling hook swing physics and mechanics.
    • The grappling hook retract physics and mechanics.
  • Level interactables: design, programming and implementation of the level interactables (wall-run objects, death areas…).
  • Level design: set the basic principles of the level design with the other designers and designed one of the levels of the game.
  • Save system programming.
  • Team support: helped the rest of the design team with Unreal Engine, GitHub and other tools.
