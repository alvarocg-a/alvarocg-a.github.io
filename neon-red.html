About the game

Are you ready to cross the red line and enter Neon Red? I worked as Game Designer and Programmer on this game at the Com JamOn.

My contributions

Designed and programmed (all in Blueprints) all the mechanics and systems, including, among others:

  1. Player core mechanics
    • The movement and control mechanics of the player.
    • The grappling hook swing physics and mechanics.
    • The grappling hook retract physics and mechanics.
  2. Level interactables: design, programming and implementation of the level interactables (wall-run objects, death areas…).
  3. Level design: set the basic principles of the level design with the other designers and designed one of the levels of the game.
  4. Save system programming.
  5. Team support: helped the rest of the design team with Unreal Engine, GitHub and other tools.
