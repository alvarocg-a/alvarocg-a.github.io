Burger Bros Circus

A 1 vs 1 multiplayer, dodgeball-style game where two gym bros must throw junk food at each other in a circus setting to make the audience laugh. Made for the Global Game Jam 2024 with the theme “Make me laugh”.

My contributions

Designed and programmed multiple mechanics and systems, including, among others:

  1. The 3Cs: the camera, the controls and the character.
  2. Shooting mechanics: the two types of shooting, the projectile trajectory preview, and the different types of ammo and level obstacles.
  3. User interface: programming and implementation of the UI.
  4. Team support for the design and art team with Unreal Engine, GitHub and other tools.
