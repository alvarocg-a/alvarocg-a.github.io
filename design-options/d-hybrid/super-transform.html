Super Transform

A first-person puzzle game, in the style of Superliminal and inspired by Zelda's Ultrahand, about transforming objects to make your way across the rooftops of a flooded, fractured Madrid under the vengeful attacks of the squids.

My contributions

Designed and programmed (all in Blueprints) all the mechanics and systems, including, among others:

  1. Player core mechanics: movement and controls, and the movement, rotation and scale of interactable objects.
  2. Level interactables: design, programming and implementation.
  3. User interface: programming and implementation of the UI.
  4. Tools for the level designers, quick to work with because we only had 48 hours: custom triggers adaptable to many circumstances, and interactables with lots of editable variables.
  5. Team support with Unreal Engine, GitHub and other tools.
