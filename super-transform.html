About the game

Super Transform is a first-person puzzle game, in the style of Superliminal and inspired by Zelda's Ultrahand, based on transforming different objects to make your way across the rooftops.

You embody an interdimensional guard whose mission is to save Madrid in a fractured reality where the city has been flooded and is under the vengeful attacks of the squids.

I worked as Game Designer and Programmer on this game at the Madrid in Game Hack Jam.

My contributions

Designed and programmed (all in Blueprints) all the mechanics and systems, including, among others:

  • Player core mechanics
    • The movement and control mechanics of the player.
    • The movement, rotation and scale of interactable objects.
  • Level interactables: design, programming and implementation.
  • User interface: programming and implementation of the UI.
  • Tools for the level designers, made to be quick and easy to work with because we only had 48 hours:
    • Custom triggers, easy for the level designer to modify and adapt to a variety of circumstances.
    • Interactables that are easy to place, with lots of editable variables to adapt to the level designer's work.
  • Team support: helped the rest of the design team with Unreal Engine, GitHub and other tools.
