About the game

In a dystopian future, Madrid has lost the most important thing, its people. With them, its music, its color and its essence are forgotten.

Recover all the Heart Beats to make Madrid beat again.

My contributions

I worked as Game Designer and Technical Designer. I designed and implemented, among others:

  1. Color recovery system: designed and programmed the system that brings the city's color back, including the shaders, the collectibles and the system that activates the color when they are picked up.
  2. Player movement: designed and implemented the character's movement.
  3. Level progression: implemented the rewards and missions that move the player around the map.
  4. Interactables & platforms: designed and implemented the game's interactables and platforms.
  5. Team support: helped the team with the development tools (Unreal Engine, GitHub and others).
